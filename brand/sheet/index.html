Brand gesture

Two overlapping rounded squares

Coral is the creator. Plum is the studio. The butter overlap is the app: what we own together. Each square is 64% of the box, offset by 36%, with corners at 20% of the box.

  • Logo mark, eyebrow bullet and section icons.
  • Zero risk: the two squares slide together and the overlap lights up.
  • Scarcity: the studio square is in place, the creator square is a dashed outline. That is an open spot.
  • Proof: "In the stores" in butter for a shipped app, "Work preview · WiP" in coral for an app in progress.
  • Build, maintain, grow: the same mark in three color orders.

Copy rules

Every line on the page

  • No em dashes. Sentences under 30 words, one idea each.
  • Data instead of adjectives. No vague words. Negations only when the meaning needs one.
  • Every section answers why the creator should care.
  • English first. Never mention code, tools, frameworks or technical work. Never name competitors.
  • Say "submitted", never "approved" or "live".
  • Every proof number has a source link and the label "reported" or "estimated". A number without a link is dropped.
