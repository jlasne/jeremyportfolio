Above the fold, the bar is plain.

One pixel of scroll and the sentinel leaves the frame. The observer flips one attribute. Everything else is CSS.

Three things change.

The ground under the bar turns clear, the pill gains a shadow, the wordmark clips down to the mark.

The call to action arrives 300ms later.
