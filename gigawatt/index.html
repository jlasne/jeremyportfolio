Gigawatt

An island, a grid, and a clock. Get to 1,000 MW of running capacity.

  1. Money buys power plants. Plants make electricity, and cost money every second to run.
  2. Electricity runs datacenters. Datacenters make tokens, and get hot doing it.
  3. Your AI model turns tokens into money. It can only digest so many, so keep it upgraded.

Running capacity is whichever side is smaller — plants alone will not do it. Two things about the land: power is wasted travelling, so build close; and water and rock keep datacenters cool, while desert bakes them.

Click a building to upgrade it. Levels roughly triple output, so once the island fills up, upgrading is the only way on.

Click a tool, then a tile. 1 and 2 pick a tool, U upgrades what you have selected, Esc puts the tool down. Laptop, keyboard and mouse.
