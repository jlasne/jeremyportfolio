Gigawatt

Build a grid to 1,000 MW. The clock starts on your first building.

  1. Money buys power plants. A plant makes megawatts and costs money every second.
  2. Drag a line from a plant to a datacenter to feed it. A line reaches 6 tiles.
  3. A fed datacenter makes tokens and heats up. Your AI model buys the tokens.

Running capacity counts the power your datacenters use. Spare power counts for 0, so every plant needs a line to somewhere.

Two land rules. A line loses 11% per tile past the third, so build close. Coolant and heat sinks cool a datacenter, the baked yard heats it.

Short of cooling? Put a fan beside a datacenter. It costs money every second and cools the 8 tiles around it.

Drag a building to empty ground to move it. Moving is free.

Click a building to upgrade it. Each level triples output and doubles what it eats.

A full run takes 26 minutes. The 10× button gives you it back in under 3, and the clock still counts game time.

Keys: 1 and 2 pick a tool. U upgrades the selected building. Esc puts the tool down. Built for a laptop and a mouse.
