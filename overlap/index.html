Why not the usual

Booking links solve the wrong half.

A link is fine when one person picks from your calendar. It falls apart the moment four people in four timezones need the same hour.

Every clock at once

One row per person, hours running across. A column is one instant read on four different clocks — no mental arithmetic, no "wait, is that my 3pm or yours?"

It has an opinion

Hours are ranked by how many can make it, then by how civil the hour is for everyone. It won't offer you 2:30am when an 8am exists.

Nobody has to sign up to be scheduled

Add a colleague by city and hours. They never need an account for the overlap to be right — invites are for people who want their own hours to follow them.

Ends in a calendar, not a maybe

One tap opens Google Calendar with the event already filled in — time, title, guests, every local time in the description. Or take the .ics.

Stop doing timezone maths in your head.

It takes about thirty seconds to see whether your team has a good hour at all.
