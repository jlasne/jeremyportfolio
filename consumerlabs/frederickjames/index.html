17 lessons you can copy, from his posts

Paywall & pricing4

  1. Use a hard paywall with weekly, monthly and yearly plans. Adding a monthly plan raised his conversion.

    “Pricing: 7.99/w & 49.99/y. Adding 14.99/m boosted conversion. Hard paywall in RevenueCat.”
    20 Jun 2026 · see the post ↗

  2. Show a tasteful exit offer, like a lifetime plan, when people abandon checkout.

    “I've made $2,700 from transaction abandon.”
    28 Sep 2026 · see the post ↗

  3. Localize pricing, screenshots and store info, then add a lifetime plan.

    “After I swapped to organic, this combo increased my revenue by about 30%.”
    14 Nov 2026 · see the post ↗

  4. Set a US base price, then scale it by each country's spending power.

    “6.99/w 14.99/m 49.99/yr 49.99/lifetime 29.99/yr exit offer”
    14 Nov 2026 · see the post ↗
