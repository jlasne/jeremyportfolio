Design style

Ladder’s app runs on near-black #0E0E0E screens, with lime #E6FF01 on buttons, done checks, progress rings and chart bars. Workout cards are full-bleed coach photos with about 18 pt corners, titled in heavy extended caps. Controls are capsules: 34 pt chips with a grey rim and a 50 pt lime pill for the main action.

Layout
16 to 19 pt side margins; a 7-day week row at a 54 pt pitch tops a swipeable 337 pt workout card.
Shapes
Chips are 34 pt capsules, the main button a 50 pt pill, day marks 27 pt circles; photo cards round at about 18 pt.
Color
Near black #0E0E0E sits behind white text; lime #E6FF01 fills the main button, done days, progress rings and chart bars.
Type
EK Modena Extended Heavy caps, tracked about 4%, set headlines and stats like 186 lbs; SF Pro sets 13 to 15 pt UI text.
Imagery
Real coach photos appear on 6 of 8 shots, darkened at the bottom under white caps titles.
Components
A 31-tick lime rep gauge, about 62 pt avatar circles in lime progress rings, and a floating glass tab bar with 4 tabs.

Copy this style

A prompt for Ladder’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in Ladder’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% white #FFFFFF: text and icons, plus onboarding and pricing pages. - 30% near black #0E0E0E: in-app screens; #1C1C1E for cards and charts. - 10% lime yellow #E6FF01: the main button, done checks, progress rings and chart bars. Type: - Headlines: Unbounded 800, close to EK Modena Extended, caps, +4% tracking, 26 to 32 pt. - Body: Inter 400, close to SF Pro, 13 to 15 pt; Inter 700 for screen titles. Shapes: - 34 pt capsule chips with a #7D7D7D rim, a 50 pt pill main button, 27 pt day circles. - Photo cards: 18 pt corners, a black gradient on the bottom third. Layout: - 16 pt side margins, 8 pt chip gaps, one workout card per view. Components: - Week row: 7 day circles, 54 pt pitch; done days fill lime with a black check. - Avatar row: 62 pt photos in lime progress rings. - Main button: full-width lime pill, black bold caps label. Icons and imagery: - White icons about 20 pt, filled in the tab bar. Real coach photos under white caps titles. Check: - In-app backgrounds are #0E0E0E or #1C1C1E. - Lime marks only actions, progress and done states. - Titles and big stats are in caps.
