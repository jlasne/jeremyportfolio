My read

  • Annual leads with $14.99 a month, half of Monthly’s $29.99; the $179.99 yearly bill sits below. Only Annual gets a dark card and lime checks.
  • Annual costs less per month and does more: every team, unlimited team changes, unlimited Save & Replay. Monthly stops at 1 team and 2 changes a month.
  • The 7-day trial asks for payment only after it ends. The App Store lists 4 tiers at $179.99 to $479.99 a year; the web shows only PRO.

Design style

Near-black app screens put lime on buttons, checks and charts, with real coach photos and heavy wide capital titles. White fills 6 of 8 store page backgrounds and the pricing page, where Annual is the one dark card.

  • White covers 34% of the store screens, near black 31%
  • 7 of 8 store headlines in heavy wide capitals
  • 3 of 3 main buttons are full lime pills

How I’d improve it

  1. Put Annual first

    Annual, the one dark card, comes third and last, under Free Trial and Monthly. Move it to the top and print the 7 free days on it.

    “Put the main actions at the top of the hierarchy, where the eye lands first.” taste.md, S2, S14, S15, S16

  2. Say what Annual saves

    Annual reads “Maximum flexibility, save monthly.” A 12-month plan is the least flexible one. Write “Half the Monthly price: save $179.89 a year”.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

  3. Use the app’s flat black

    The pricing page uses 2 grey gradients: Annual runs #1B1B1B to #474747, Monthly #B6B6B6 to #FAFAFA. Fill Annual with the app’s flat #0E0E0E and the other cards with white.

    “Avoid many shades and opacities of the same color. Pick 3 to 5 neutrals and reuse them.” taste.md, S1

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Ladder’s style: a sticky header with the logo and a lime pill button, then 2 stacked cards, Annual first. Each card: bold caps plan name, huge price, small grey “/ month”, one line of copy, checklist. Annual is the one dark card: “7 days free” on top, $14.99 a month, $179.99 a year below, lime checks. Type: Archivo at weight 900 and width 125 for headlines and prices, Inter for body. Colors: white #FFFFFF (60) page, near black #0E0E0E (30) type and Annual, lime yellow #E6FF01 (10) button and checks. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
