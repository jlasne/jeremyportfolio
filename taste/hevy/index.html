My read

  • Yearly comes preselected at $23.99, or $2.00 a month: 33% under 12 months of the $2.99 Monthly, 50% under the App Store’s $3.99 Monthly.
  • Lifetime, $74.99, costs as much as 3.1 years of Yearly. It sits right under the selected plan and makes $23.99 look small.
  • Free logs unlimited workouts but stops at 4 routines, 7 custom exercises and 3 months of graphs. Pro lifts those caps and adds Hevy Trainer.

Design style

White screens, near-black text and one blue, #267EE8, on buttons, links, charts and the band under each store shot. The web paywall swaps in a brighter blue, #008CFF, and adds a yellow PRO label, #F7D000.

  • Near-white covers 75% of the screens
  • A blue band fills the bottom 23% of all 8 store shots
  • Corners about 8 px on the paywall card and button

How I’d improve it

  1. Print $2.00 a month on Yearly

    Monthly and Yearly each repeat the period: “Billed monthly”, “Billed yearly”. On the preselected Yearly row, swap “Billed yearly” for “$2.00 a month, save 33%”.

    “Drop redundant labels. If the value explains itself, remove the label.” taste.md, S2, S4, S14, S15

  2. Take the paywall text to 4.5:1

    Yellow PRO, #F7D000 on the #F7F9FB page, measures 1.4:1, and white on the #008CFF button 3.4:1. Cut PRO from the 3 rows, since all are Pro, and darken the blue to #0072D6 for 4.8:1.

    “Text contrast: at least 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for text 24 px and larger.” taste.md, S27

  3. Name what Pro unlocks

    “Take your training to the next level” heads the Free vs PRO table. Write what the table shows: “Unlock Hevy Trainer, unlimited routines and your full graph history”.

    “Make headlines specific. ‘Track 3 habits in 10 seconds a day’ beats ‘Transform your life’.” taste.md, S1, S2, S4, S16

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Hevy’s style. Top to bottom: a logo bar, a semibold headline, a one-line subline, then 3 radio rows: Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime. Preselect Yearly with a 2 px blue outline and a filled radio. Each row: plan name in blue, full price below, billing line at right. Then a full-width blue button and a Free vs Pro table with blue checks. Type: Figtree, semibold headline, regular prices. Colors: white #FFFFFF (60) for the page, black #000000 (30) for text, bright blue #267EE8 (10) for the outline, button and checks. Show full prices only and skip the trial.
