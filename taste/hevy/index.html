Design style

Hevy is a white, list-first tracker: near-black text, grey #F3F4F8 secondary buttons and white cards with 1 pt #EDEDED borders. One blue, #267EE8, covers main buttons, links, selected chips and every chart. Done sets turn light green #C7FEA0, and store shots stack heavy all-caps headlines over a blue band.

Layout
17 pt side margins; routine cards hold a title, a 2-line grey summary and a full-width blue button.
Shapes
Buttons are about 40 pt tall with 10 pt corners; cards round at about 8 pt; chips and bars are full pills.
Color
White #FFFFFF screens, black #000000 text, blue #267EE8 for main buttons, links and charts; done sets fill #C7FEA0 with a #56BD23 check.
Type
Inter ExtraBold 800 caps set store headlines; SemiBold 600 sets titles; Regular 400 sets 16 pt rows and grey sublines.
Imagery
Exercise rows show grey 3D figures with red target muscles on 4 of 8 shots; avatars are round photos, 88 pt on profiles.
Components
Set rows with previous, kg, reps and a 25 pt check box; blue line and bar charts; 12.5 pt round-ended comparison bars.

Copy this style

A prompt for Hevy’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in Hevy’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% white #FFFFFF: every screen and card; secondary buttons #F3F4F8; borders #EDEDED. - 30% black #000000: titles, values and icons; secondary text #86898E. - 10% bright blue #267EE8: main buttons, links, selected chips, the active tab, charts. - Done states: row #C7FEA0, check box #56BD23. Type: - Headlines: Inter 800 caps for heroes; Inter 600 at about 18 pt for titles. - Body: Inter 400, 16 pt rows, 13 pt grey sublines; tabular numbers. Shapes: - Buttons 40 pt tall with 10 pt corners; cards 8 pt corners with a 1 pt #EDEDED border; chips and bars are full pills. Layout: - 17 pt side margins; lists sit in bordered white cards. Components: - Routine card: title, a 3-dot menu, 2-line grey summary, full-width blue button. - Set row: previous, kg, reps and a 25 pt check box; a done row turns #C7FEA0. - Charts: thin blue line with dot markers, or blue bars on light grid lines. Icons and imagery: - Outline icons in black; grey 3D muscle figures with red target muscles. Check: - Blue marks every main button, link and chart. - Every screen background is white. - Buttons are 40 pt tall with 10 pt corners.
