    4 rules every demo follows

    From S2, S4 and S14.

    • Animations must be interruptible mid-way

      Tap any demo while it moves. It turns around from where it is.

    • Stop looping animations

      Loop only a loading state. Each demo on this page runs once per tap.

    • Animate useful feedback

      Success, error, progress. The pulse, the banner and the sheet each answer an action.

    • Respect Reduce Motion

      Swap movement for fades. This page follows your device setting. Try it here.

    Paste the values into your app

    The lime button copies these CSS custom properties plus the 24 rules as a list. Paste both into your project or your prompt.
