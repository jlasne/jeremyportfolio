Design style

Huckleberry sets slate navy #45526A text on white screens, with color-coded logs: cyan sleep, orange feeds, yellow diapers and purple pumping. Controls are circles, pills and 34 pt segmented bars, and green #8FD33D marks the selected segment and the Save button. Store shots float white phones over ice-white #F3FFFF and aqua backdrops.

Layout
15 to 19 pt side margins; forms stack labeled sections split by thin rules, with a full-width Save pill at the bottom.
Shapes
Segmented controls round at 4.2 pt, choice buttons are about 80 pt circles, and Save is a full-width pill.
Color
Slate navy #45526A text on white; cyan #36D2E9 sleep cards and charts; orange #FD763F feeds; green #8FD33D selections and Save.
Type
One unnamed geometric sans with a single-story a, close to Lexend: 23 pt Medium titles, 16 pt Light tabs.
Imagery
White phones sit on aqua backdrops with 4-point sparkles; tip cards use flat figure drawings, food logs use cut-out photos.
Components
Color-coded log cards, a 167 pt cyan sleep donut with 2 pt gaps, and 138 pt orange LEFT and RIGHT nursing timer buttons.
