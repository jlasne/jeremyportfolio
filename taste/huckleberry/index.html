My read

  • Yearly opens first and crosses out the monthly price: $14.99 sits above $9.99 a month, billed yearly, though $119.99 / 12 is $10.00. Premium’s year reads as 33% off.
  • Plus is the decoy on Monthly: $11.99, $3 under Premium’s $14.99. On Yearly it turns into the bargain, $5.74 a month, 52% off against Premium’s 33%.
  • Free keeps every log and caregiver sync at $0. Plus sells SweetSpot nap predictions for $5.74 a month; Premium adds Berry AI chat and sleep plans for $9.99 a month.

Design style

Aqua and white store screens with slate navy headlines; inside the app, color-coded cards mark each log type: cyan sleep, orange feeds, yellow diapers, purple pumping. The web paywall switches to warm off-white #F7F6F2, with white plan cards, cyan pill buttons and a lime-to-green outline on Premium.

  • Cyan backdrops on 4 of the first 8 store shots
  • Slate navy #44526C sets the first 8 store headlines
  • Plan cards round at 16 px; buttons are full pills

How I’d improve it

  1. Set button labels in dark navy

    White “Get started” and “Get the App” read at 1.8:1 on the #36D2E9 cyan. Keep the cyan and set the labels in #2B3648, the navy of the SweetSpot alert: 6.7:1.

    “Text contrast: at least 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for text 24 px and larger.” taste.md, S27

  2. Keep one filled button per screen

    The first screen shows 2 filled cyan pills, “Get the App” and “Get started”, and the Free card fills a third. Make “Get the App” a text link and give Free the outline of “Learn more”.

    “Emphasize the CTA. One filled primary button, everything else secondary or text.” taste.md, S2, S14, S15, S16

  3. Put SweetSpot and $5.74 in the headline

    The headline reads “Support that fits your family”. Name the feature the store description calls most-loved, with the entry price: “SweetSpot nap predictions from $5.74 a month”.

    “Make headlines specific. ‘Track 3 habits in 10 seconds a day’ beats ‘Transform your life’.” taste.md, S1, S2, S4, S16

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Huckleberry’s style. Top to bottom: a centered headline and a Yearly and Monthly pill toggle. Below: stacked white plan cards with 16 px corners, priciest first, free last, each with a full-width pill button. Type: Avenir or Nunito Sans, heavy headlines and prices, regular body, in slate navy #44526C. Colors: ice white #F3FFFF (60) for the page, berry cyan #31BCD3 (30) for buttons with navy labels, leaf lime #CDCC04 (10) for the top plan’s outline. Open on Yearly: cross out the monthly price above the per-month price. Show a trial only if you offer one. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
