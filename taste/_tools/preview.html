Our read

  • The yearly plan is $5.00 a month, a third of the monthly price. The weekly plan makes it look cheap.
  • Every price ends in .99, and the lifetime plan costs 2.5 years of the yearly plan.
  • The trial sits on the yearly plan only, so the default choice is also the biggest payment.

What we’d improve

  1. Make the yearly plan the one bright block

    All 3 rows share one orange outline. Fill only the yearly row, so the eye lands on the plan the app wants.

    “The 10% is what people click: buttons, the product label, the main number.” taste.md, S31

  2. Raise the contrast of the plan prices

    The per-week prices sit in light grey on white. Set them in the same ink as the plan names.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

  3. Keep orange for the main button

    Orange fills the hero, the badge and the button. Move the hero to white and let the button carry the color.

    “Pick the accent for a reason, not because you like it.” taste.md, S23
