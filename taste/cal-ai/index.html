Design style

Cal AI keeps white screens and ink-black #1D1A23 type, with floating white cards on soft grey shadows. Ink black also fills every main button, the progress rings and the add button, so color appears only in food photos and data: macros, streak and progress. A floating 62 pt capsule tab bar and big Semibold numbers like 1250 define the home screen.

Layout
24 pt side margins; the home screen stacks a week row, a 345 pt calorie card, 3 macro cards and recent meals.
Shapes
Cards round at about 16 to 20 pt over soft grey shadows; buttons and the tab bar are capsules 48 to 62 pt tall.
Color
White #FFFFFF screens; ink black #1D1A23 for text, rings and main buttons; grey #7F7F80 for labels and inactive tabs.
Type
A neutral sans close to Inter: Semibold 600 for 38 pt numbers like 1250, Regular for 11 to 15 pt grey labels.
Imagery
Real food photos appear on 4 of 6 shots; 1 shot compares before and after body photos.
Components
A 62 pt floating capsule tab bar plus a black add button, 90 pt calorie rings, and paired 48 pt pills.
