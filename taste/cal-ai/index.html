My read

  • One tier, Unlimited, sold at 5 prices from $2.99 to $29.99 across 9 listings. The names hide each plan’s length, so the ladder only reads inside the app.
  • Hard paywall on the core feature: the description says scan results require a subscription. The lifestyle quiz comes first, so the price lands after you build a plan.
  • Streak Restore, $0.99, sells a lost streak back. The flame count shows on Home, Progress and Groups, so a missed day becomes a sale.

Design style

White screens, near-black type and floating white cards with soft shadows, built around real food photos. Color appears only on data: red, orange and blue macros and the orange streak.

  • White fills 70% of the screenshots
  • Cards round at about 16 px, buttons are full pills

How I’d improve it

  1. Show 1 scan result before the price

    The description says scan results require a subscription, so people subscribe before they see one result. Let the first photo return its full breakdown, then show the plans.

    “Onboarding before sign-up. Order: show the value, let them personalize, then ask them to sign up.” taste.md, S13

  2. Name each plan by its length

    The App Store lists 9 items named Unlimited or Unlimited Plan, from $2.99 to $29.99. Add the length to each name, like “Unlimited, 1 year”, so each price reads before install.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

  3. Flatten the Weight Progress card

    On Progress, the chart card holds 3 boxes: a bordered goal pill, a grey range switch and a green tip box. Keep the switch and set the goal and the tip as plain text.

    “Flatten nested cards. Avoid a card inside a card inside a card.” taste.md, S14

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Cal AI’s style. Top to bottom: a semibold 2-line headline, a white card with one big number and 3 macro rings, the plans, one black pill button. Type: Inter, semibold headlines, bold numbers, regular grey labels. Colors: white #FFFFFF (60), ink black #1D1A23 (30) for text and the button, grey #7F7F80 (10) for small marks. Cards: 16 px corners, soft shadow. Plans: one tier, Unlimited, as rows with length and price; preselect the longest with a black outline and show its per-month price. Show a trial only if you offer one. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
