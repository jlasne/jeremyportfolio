Design style

Near-black #141414 screens carry white text, with #21211F for the tab bar, nav bar and sheets. Orange #FD5200 marks only the active state: the current tab, selected chips, routes and chart lines. Screens pack data tight: 4 stats per row, 34 pt outlined chips, photos at full content width and dark maps.

Layout
24 pt side margins; activity stats sit in 4 columns, an 11 pt label over a 17 pt value.
Shapes
Chips: 34 pt tall, 1 pt #434240 outline, 6 pt corners, full pills over maps; photos round at about 8 pt.
Color
#141414 screens and #21211F bars carry white text; orange #FD5200 marks only the active tab, selected chips, routes and the Save button.
Type
Boathouse Extended Bold sets store headlines; Boathouse Bold sets 24 pt stats like 384.6 mi; Regular sets 11 to 14 pt labels.
Imagery

A 5-tab bar on #21211F with the active icon filled orange; 4-column stat rows; charts draw a 2.5 pt orange line.
