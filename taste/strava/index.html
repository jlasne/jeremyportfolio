My read

  • Strava + Runna comes first at $12.50 a month, so Individual at $6.67 looks like half price. Only Individual gets a light card, an orange button and “Save 44%”.
  • Each card leads with a per-month price; 3 of the 4 bill yearly only. Family turns $139.99 a year into $2.92 a month per person.
  • The trial runs 30 days, with a reminder 2 days before it ends. Individual and Student say “Try it free”; Family and the bundle say “Choose plan”.

How I’d improve it

  1. Keep one orange button on screen

    On the Individual card, “Try it free” sits above the sticky “Start free 30-day trial”: 2 orange buttons on one screen. Make the card button an outline, like the other 3 cards.

    “Emphasize the CTA. One filled primary button, everything else secondary or text.” taste.md, S2, S14, S15, S16

  2. Name what the trial unlocks

    The headline reads “The best of Strava. Built for your goals.” The timeline under it names the features: put them up top, as “Routes, segment leaderboards and training analysis, free for 30 days”.

    “Make headlines specific. ‘Track 3 habits in 10 seconds a day’ beats ‘Transform your life’.” taste.md, S1, S2, S4, S16

  3. Write plan names in sentence case

    All 4 plan names are spaced capitals ending in “SUBSCRIPTION”, and 3 of them take 2 lines. Set them as Individual, Family, Student and Strava + Runna, and each fits on 1 line.

    “Use ALL CAPS only for tiny labels, with +5% to +10% letter spacing.” taste.md, S1, S2, S4, S16

Copy this style

Build a dark paywall in Strava’s style. Top to bottom: a red-to-black gradient hero with the logo and a bold 2-line headline, then a white trial timeline. Below: 4 stacked plan cards and a sticky full-width button. Type: Archivo, bold headlines, regular prices at 4x the body size. Colors: near black #141414 (60) for the page and cards, white #FFFFFF (30) for text, Strava orange #FD5200 (10) for the sticky button only. Put the priciest plan first as an anchor. Make the main plan the one light card: Annual preselected, price per month, yearly bill below, 30 days free. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
