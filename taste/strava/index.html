Design style

Copy this style

A prompt for Strava’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in Strava’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% near black #141414: screen backgrounds; #21211F for bars and sheets. - 30% white #FFFFFF: text and icons; secondary text 75% white. - 10% Strava orange #FD5200: only the active tab, selected chips, main buttons, routes and chart lines. Type: - Headlines: Montserrat 700, close to Boathouse Extended, about 28 to 32 pt. - Body: Inter 400, close to Boathouse, 13 to 14 pt; Inter 700 for 17 pt titles and 24 pt stats. Shapes: - Chips 34 pt tall, 6 pt corners, 1 pt #434240 outline; selected chips get an orange outline and label. - Photos 8 pt corners. Flat surfaces with 1 pt #3A3A38 dividers. Layout: - 24 pt side margins, 16 pt between blocks, dense data rows. Components: - Tab bar: 5 outlined white icons; the active one fills orange. - Stat row: 4 columns, 11 pt label over 17 pt value. - Charts: 2.5 pt orange line, 26% orange fill, 20% white gridlines. Icons and imagery: - 2 pt line icons, round caps; dark maps with orange routes; real photos at full width. Check: - Every screen background is #141414. - Orange appears only on active or selected items. - Stats read label over value, 4 per row.
