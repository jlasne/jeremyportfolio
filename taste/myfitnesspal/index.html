My read

  • Monthly, $19.99, anchors Annual: 12 months cost $239.88, so $79.99 saves 67%. A year also costs “about the same as 1 hour with a nutrition expert”.
  • All 5 buttons say START FREE TRIAL, and 4 open the Annual checkout, as does “Claim offer”. Monthly gets one outlined button.
  • One tier, 4 prices: the web sells $79.99 a year and $19.99 a month; the App Store adds $49.99 a year and $9.99 a month.

Design style

Store screens put white app UI on a warm stone photo under bold white headlines, and blue #0066EE fills the icon and the buttons. The web paywall flips it: blue fills the page, and yellow #FDDA1C marks the tags, checks and buttons.

  • 8 of 8 store screens sit on warm stone photos
  • Plan cards round at about 35 px; buttons are 44 px pills
  • 1 yellow, #FDDA1C, on the paywall tags, checks and buttons

How I’d improve it

  1. Put the 7 days on the button

    All 5 buttons say “START FREE TRIAL”; the 7 days sit in the top banner and in a closed FAQ answer. Print “Start 7 days free” on the Annual button and “Then $79.99 a year” under it.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

  2. Trade both badges for $6.67 a month

    The same Annual card says “Best value!” under the 8/10, then “Most popular!” in the plan picker. Swap both tags for its monthly cost, $6.67, next to Monthly’s $19.99.

    “Delete filler badges (‘New’, ‘AI-powered’, ‘Beta’) unless they change a decision.” taste.md, S14

  3. Fill the plan sections with flat blue

    The plan sections fade from the icon blue, #0066EE, to lavender #8A81FA at the bottom left. Fill them with flat #0066EE, so yellow stays the one bright color.

    “Gradient backgrounds” taste.md, S1

Copy this style

Build a paywall in MyFitnessPal’s style. Top to bottom: a bold headline, then a rounded card per plan, Annual first. Each card holds the plan name, 3 checked features, a big yearly or monthly price, a billing line and a full-width pill button. Tag and outline Annual, and add “67% savings over the Monthly Plan”. Monthly gets an outlined button. Every button starts a 7-day free trial. Type: Inter, bold headlines at -2% tracking, regular body. Colors: white #FFFFFF (60) for cards, stone grey #746A61 (30) for the page, bright blue #0066EE (10) for the tag, outline and buttons. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
