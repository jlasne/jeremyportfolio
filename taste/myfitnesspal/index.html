Design style

MyFitnessPal stacks white cards with 15 pt corners and 24 pt padding on light screens under a pale blue-grey header tint. Bright blue #0066EE fills every main button as a full pill, while teal, purple and orange code carbs, fat and protein. Store shots set the app on a warm stone photo under bold white headlines.

Layout
16 pt screen margins; 361 pt cards with 24 pt padding and 12 pt gaps; macros sit in 3 columns of 94 pt.
Shapes
Cards round at 15 pt and fields at 6 pt with 2 pt borders; buttons are 48 pt pills, progress bars 8 pt.
Color
White cards on #F6F7F9 sheets; blue #0066EE on buttons and goals; macros in teal #2CB9B1, purple #6C0D8F and orange #FEB13D.
Type
Inter Bold headlines tracked 3% tight; in-app values like 598 cal set 20 pt Bold over 14 pt Regular labels.
Imagery
All 8 store shots sit on a warm stone photo; food photos appear on 4 of 8, from salads to recipe cards.
Components
A 7-day check row, a calorie card with a 314 pt blue bar, stacked macro bars, and white food rows with green checks.

Copy this style

A prompt for MyFitnessPal’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in MyFitnessPal’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% white #FFFFFF: cards, sheets and screens below the header. - 30% stone grey #746A61: hero and onboarding backdrops, as in the store shots. - 10% bright blue #0066EE: main buttons, links, goal values and the calorie bar. - Macros: carbs #2CB9B1, fat #6C0D8F, protein #FEB13D. Secondary text #6C6C70. Type: - Headlines: Inter 700, about 28 to 46 pt, tracking -3%. - Body: Inter 400, 14 pt; values Inter 700, 20 pt, units 12 pt grey. Shapes: - Cards 15 pt corners; fields 6 pt with a 2 pt #BEC5CF border; buttons 48 pt pills; bars 8 pt pills. Layout: - 16 pt margins, 24 pt card padding, 12 pt gaps; a #CDD9E2 tint behind the header. Components: - Week row: 7 circles; done days fill black with a white check. - Calorie card: label, 20 pt value, “/ goal” in grey, full-width 8 pt blue bar. - Food row: name, green check, “kcal, portion” in grey, red trash icon at right. Icons and imagery: - Thin line icons; real food photos with 12 pt corners. Check: - Every main button is a #0066EE pill. - Macro colors match on every screen. - Cards sit 12 pt apart with 24 pt padding.

My read

  • Monthly, $19.99, anchors Annual: 12 months cost $239.88, so $79.99 saves 67%. A year also costs “about the same as 1 hour with a nutrition expert”.
  • All 5 buttons say START FREE TRIAL, and 4 open the Annual checkout, as does “Claim offer”. Monthly gets one outlined button.
  • The App Store text names 2 tiers, Premium and Premium Plus, but all 10 listed items are Premium. The web sells $79.99 a year and $19.99 a month; the App Store adds $49.99 a year and $9.99 a month.
