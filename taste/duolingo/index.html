Design style

Duolingo sets dark grey #4B4B4B text on white screens, where answer cards, word chips and number tiles stand on a 4 to 6 pt bottom edge, like keys. Owl green #57CC02 fills progress bars and correct answers, streak orange #FF9600 marks the streak, and each subject owns a caption color. Cartoon characters appear on 5 of 8 shots, and every store caption sits in a lowercase colored bar.

Layout
16 to 28 pt side margins; lesson screens put a 17 pt progress bar on top and a 2-column grid of answer cards below.
Shapes
Cards, chips and tiles: 10 to 13 pt corners, 2 pt #E5E5E5 borders, a 4 to 6 pt bottom edge; bars are full pills.
Color
White #FFFFFF screens and #4B4B4B text; owl green #57CC02 for progress and correct answers; streak orange #FF9600 for the streak.
Type
Feather Bold sets lowercase store captions; DIN Next Rounded Bold sets prompts and big numbers like 36; Regular sets 20 pt chips.
Imagery
Flat cartoon characters appear on 5 of 8 shots; answer art is flat objects, like a teacup and a milk carton.
Components
A 7-day streak card with 33.5 pt check circles, a word bank of 45 pt tiles, and a 17 pt green progress bar.

Copy this style

A prompt for Duolingo’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in Duolingo’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% white #FFFFFF: every screen and card; text #4B4B4B; borders #E5E5E5. - 30% owl green #57CC02: progress bars, correct answers and success states. - 10% streak orange #FF9600: the streak count and done days. - One bright color per section header bar. Type: - Headlines: Livvic 700, close to Feather Bold, lowercase, about 36 pt. - Body: Barlow 700, close to DIN Next Rounded, 16 to 20 pt prompts; Barlow 500 for 20 pt chips. Shapes: - Cards, chips and buttons: 10 to 13 pt corners, 2 pt #E5E5E5 border, 4 to 6 pt darker bottom edge. - Progress bars: 17 pt pills. Layout: - 16 pt side margins; one question per screen; answers in a 2-column grid or chip bank. Components: - Answer card: white, grey key edge; selected turns light blue with a blue border. - Word chip: 45 pt tall, 16 pt side padding; correct: #D7FEB7 fill, #A6EC70 border. - Streak week: 7 circles, 33.5 pt; done days orange with white checks. Icons and imagery: - Flat cartoon characters and flat object art; bold rounded icons. Check: - Every tappable card has a 4 to 6 pt bottom edge. - Headlines are lowercase. - Green marks only progress and correct answers.
