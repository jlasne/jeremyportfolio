My read

  • Every course is free, and an energy meter sells Super. Lesson screens show it falling 25, 24, 23, 22; Super’s perks include Unlimited Energy.
  • Super starts with 14 days free. Monthly Streak Repair ties it to the streak counter, so a missed day becomes a reason to pay.
  • 6 Super listings run from $9.99 to $119.99. Gems add a second ladder: 1,200 for $4.99 up to 6,500 for $19.99, which gives 35% more gems per dollar.

Design style

White screens with dark grey type, where answer cards, word chips and number tiles stand on a thicker bottom edge, like keys. Cartoon characters appear on 5 of 8 screens, and chess, math and music each own a color: teal, blue, purple.

  • White covers 68% of the screenshots
  • Answer cards: 2 px border, 6 px bottom edge
  • Corners about 12 px on cards, chips and tiles

How I’d improve it

  1. Name each Super listing by its length

    The App Store shows 6 items named Super Duolingo, from $9.99 to $119.99. Add the length to each name, like “Super Duolingo, 1 year”, so shoppers can compare prices before install.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

  2. Show energy in grey until it runs low

    Lesson screens put a pink energy meter next to the progress bar, so 2 colors compete above every question. Draw energy in grey #4B4B4B and turn it pink under 5, when Unlimited Energy starts to matter.

    “One accent per screen. Directing attention means one place to look.” taste.md, S8

  3. Darken the caption fills to pass 3:1

    7 of 8 captions put white text on bright fills at 2.1 to 2.5:1. Fill each with the darker shade of its bottom edge, like #CE6603 under the orange, which reads at 3.8:1.

    “Text contrast: at least 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for text 24 px and larger.” taste.md, S27

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Duolingo’s style. Top to bottom: a mascot, a lowercase 2-line headline, 3 perks with icons, the plans, one full-width button. Type: Fredoka 600 for headlines, M PLUS Rounded 1c for body, dark grey #4B4B4B text. Colors: white #FFFFFF (60) for the screen, owl green #57CC02 (30) for the mascot and perk icons, streak orange #FF9600 (10) for the button. Cards and buttons: 12 px corners, 2 px border, 6 px darker bottom edge. Plans: one tier in rows with length and price; preselect yearly and show its per-month price. Put the 14-day trial on the button. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
