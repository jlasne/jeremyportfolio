Design style

Opal sets frosted-glass cards on near-black #0E0F11 screens, lit by ice-blue #B4D4E6 numbers, rings and glows. Cards round at 33 pt continuous corners with a faint white rim, and every button is a glass capsule 42 to 51 pt tall. Photo-real 3D stones and gems crown the hero screens and float in the store art.

Layout
Screens center one hero, a 293 pt ring or a 3D gem, above glass cards with 20 pt inner padding.
Shapes
Glass cards: 33 pt continuous corners, a white rim at 18%; buttons and value pills: full capsules 42 to 51 pt tall.
Color
Near black #0E0F11 behind #EEEEEE titles and #BCBBC0 body text; ice blue #B4D4E6 and a #819DC4 to #9FF8FE gradient mark numbers and rings.
Type
SF Pro mixes Medium and Bold words in one headline at 4% tight tracking; 12 to 14 pt spaced caps label the stats.
Imagery
Photo-real 3D stones, gems and rock arches appear on 6 of 8 shots, over misty ice-blue backdrops.
Components
A 293 pt screen-time ring with a 22.5 pt ice-blue arc, glass app tiles, and a capsule stepper with 37 pt round buttons.

A prompt for Opal’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in Opal’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% near black #0E0F11: screen backgrounds, with a #495C6B glow at the top. - 30% soft white #EEEEEE: titles and icons; body text #BCBBC0. - 10% ice blue #B4D4E6: key numbers, rings, active icons; arcs fade #819DC4 to #9FF8FE. Type: - Headlines: Inter 700, close to SF Pro, 28 pt, tracking -4%, key words Bold inside Inter 500 lines. - Body: Inter 500, close to SF Pro, 13 to 15 pt; stat labels about 12 pt caps, tracking 5%. Shapes: - Glass cards: background blur, white rim at 18%, 33 pt continuous corners. - Buttons and steppers: capsules 42 to 51 pt tall. Layout: - 32 pt side margins, one centered hero per screen, 20 pt card padding. Components: - Ring: 293 pt, 22.5 pt round-capped arc on a #151515 track, 55 pt value centered. - Stat row: 3 columns, caps label over an ice-blue number of about 28 pt. - Glass button: 42 pt capsule, white label, ice-blue glow on the bottom rim. Icons and imagery: - White rounded line icons; one photo-real 3D stone or gem per hero. Check: - Every background is #0E0F11 or its top glow. - Cards show the background through frosted glass. - Headlines mix 2 weights.
