My read

  • Yearly opens preselected at $8.29 a month. Monthly shows $19.99 and “$239 billed annually”, so $99.99 a year reads as 58% off.
  • The trial follows the plan: 7 days on Yearly, 3 on Monthly, and Lifetime goes straight to “Purchase”. The default plan gets the longest trial.
  • Lifetime at $399, 4 times the yearly price, anchors the top of the ladder. The App Store also lists weekly plans at $4.99 and $9.99.

Design style

Black screens with frosted-glass cards, ice-blue numbers and photo-real 3D stones. The web paywall keeps the black but swaps the accent for a purple-to-pink gradient.

  • Black covers 88% of the paywall
  • 8 of 8 store headlines mix bold and regular
  • Card corners about 28 pt

How I’d improve it

  1. Put the app’s ice blue on the button

    The button (#C08CF4 to #EDB2B2) and the PRO badge use a purple-to-pink gradient. The app marks its numbers and icons in ice blue (#B4D4E6). Use it on the button with near-black text, at 12:1 contrast, and make the badge grey.

    “Pick the accent for a reason, not because you like it. It must contrast with the base pair and fit the brand mood.” taste.md, S23

  2. Lead with the 1h 23m

    The card promises “Level up your focus”. Screenshot 1 and the App Store text promise 1h 23m back every day. Put “Get back 1h 23m a day” under the Opal PRO title.

    “Make headlines specific. ‘Track 3 habits in 10 seconds a day’ beats ‘Transform your life’.” taste.md, S1, S2, S4, S16

  3. Say how Monthly bills

    The Monthly tab reads “$239 billed annually”, but the plan bills $19.99 a month. Write “Billed monthly, $239.88 a year” and add “Save 58%” to the Yearly tab.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

Copy this style

Build a dark paywall like Opal’s. Top: an award badge and the rating. Below, one card holds the app name with a PRO badge, a one-line promise and 3 tabs: Yearly, Monthly, Lifetime. Under the tabs: one big price, the billed total in small grey text, 5 checked features and a full-width pill button. Preselect Yearly and show its price per month. Give Yearly a 7-day trial and Monthly 3 days; Lifetime is a one-time purchase. Type: SF Pro, bold price, regular body. Colors: near black #0E0F11 (60%), soft white #EEEEEE (30%), ice blue #B4D4E6 (10%) on the button. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
