My read

  • Annual opens preselected in a yellow card at $34.99, beside a struck $69.99. Monthly, $12.99, is the anchor: 12 months cost $155.88, so full-price Annual saves 55%.
  • The App Store offers a free trial. This web offer swaps it for 50% off year one: $34.99, then $69.99 a year.
  • The App Store lists 10 items at 3 prices under 4 names. “Headspace Plus” costs $12.99 or $94.99, exactly like “Headspace”, so it is 1 tier.

Design style

Yellow and orange waves frame white app screens, and a smiling orange dot is the mascot. Blue fills the Play buttons; the web paywall marks the chosen plan with a yellow card.

  • Yellow tops 6 of 8 App Store screenshots
  • 5 of 8 store headlines set key words in orange

How I’d improve it

  1. Name what the year buys

    The top line reads “Limited time offer”. Swap it for what the year buys, with numbers from the App Store text: “500+ guided meditations, from 3 minutes”.

    “Make headlines specific. ‘Track 3 habits in 10 seconds a day’ beats ‘Transform your life’.” taste.md, S1, S2, S4, S16

  2. Say 50% off once

    The first screen says “50% off” 3 times: in the headline, the blue badge and the fine print, beside a struck $69.99. Drop the badge: the struck price already shows the deal.

    “Delete filler badges (‘New’, ‘AI-powered’, ‘Beta’) unless they change a decision.” taste.md, S14

  3. Keep the bright colors to 2

    Yellow, blue, orange and green share one white page. Keep the yellow card and the blue button. Set the orange links and the green check in the headline’s grey #44423F: the links go from 3.3:1 to 10:1.

    “Two colors do 90% of the work. The third one only goes on the button you want pressed.” taste.md, S5

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Headspace’s style. Top to bottom: the logo, a bold 2-line offer headline, 2 stacked plan cards, a grey renewal line, 2 links and a full-width pill button. Type: Hanken Grotesk, close to Headspace Apercu: bold headline and prices, regular labels. Colors: white #FFFFFF (60) for the page, orange #FF7300 (30) for the logo and selected card, blue #1A54F6 (10) for the button only. Preselect Annual with its first-year price beside the struck full price. Monthly sits below, outlined. Price Annual per year and Monthly per month. Offer 50% off year one, then the full yearly price. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
