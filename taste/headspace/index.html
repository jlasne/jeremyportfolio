Design style

Headspace pairs white screens and warm grey #2B2A28 text with rounded vector art: smiling suns, soft circles and stripes in orange, yellow, pink and blue. Controls are full pills, from the 52 pt blue #0061EE Play button to the #F8F4F1 teacher row. Sleep content flips to navy #101340, and store shots frame the app in yellow and orange waves.

Layout
24 pt side margins; content pages open on a full-width art header, then an about 30 pt title, a meta line and a paragraph.
Shapes
Buttons and choice rows are full pills, 50 to 52 pt tall; category tiles and cards round at about 12 pt.
Color
White #FFFFFF screens, warm grey #2B2A28 text, blue #0061EE Play buttons; sleep screens turn navy #101340; orange #FF7300 leads the art.
Type
Headspace Apercu Bold, tracked 3% tight, sets titles; Medium 500 sets 16 to 20 pt labels; Regular sets 19 pt chat text.
Imagery
Vector art of smiling suns, circles and stripes fills 7 of 8 shots; 1 shot shows a therapist photo.
Components
2-column category tiles 167 pt wide, a teacher pick row with a blue radio, and a 52 pt blue Play pill.

Copy this style

A prompt for Headspace’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in Headspace’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% white #FFFFFF: screens and cards; text #2B2A28; soft fills #F8F4F1. - 30% bright orange #FF7300: illustrations and key words in headlines. - 10% cobalt blue #1A54F6: therapy screens; main buttons use its twin #0061EE. - Sleep screens: navy #101340 with white text. Type: - Headlines: Hanken Grotesk 700, close to Headspace Apercu, about 30 pt, tracking -3%. - Body: Hanken Grotesk 400, close to Headspace Apercu, 16 pt; labels at 500, 16 to 20 pt. Shapes: - Buttons and choice rows: full pills, 50 to 52 pt tall. Cards and tiles: 12 pt corners. - Secondary buttons: white, 2 pt #E2DEDB border, 2 pt bottom lip #D2D1CF. Layout: - 24 pt side margins; full-width art header, then title, meta, paragraph, actions. Components: - Play button: #0061EE pill, white triangle and label. - Choice row: #F8F4F1 pill, 38 pt avatar, label, 24 pt blue radio at right. - Category tiles: 2 columns, saturated bases, clipped circles, centered white labels. Icons and imagery: - Vector art of circles, suns and stripes; white round icon buttons over art. Check: - Every primary action is a blue pill. - Text is #2B2A28 on white or white on navy. - Cards and tiles round at 12 pt.
