My read

Design style

Black app screens where violet marks tabs and tags and orange marks energy numbers, framed by a violet-to-orange sunrise on every store screen. The web paywall switches to white, with a violet button, a green coupon card and a red countdown.

  • Near black covers 42% of the store screens

How I’d improve it

  1. Keep violet as the only accent

    Red, green and violet compete above the plans: a red #ED4443 banner, a green #95F3AB coupon card and the violet plan. The sticky bar already shows the timer, so drop the banner, grey the coupon and keep violet for the choice.

    “One accent per screen. Directing attention means one place to look.” taste.md, S8

  2. Show the ideal bedtime

    The plan screen blurs its one personal result, the ideal bedtime, above “Your personalized energy & sleep plan is ready”. Show the time, then sell the energy schedule behind it: the peaks and dips from the App Store screens.

    “Onboarding before sign-up. Order: show the value, let them personalize, then ask them to sign up.” taste.md, S13

  3. Put the renewal price on the card

    Quarterly leads with $0.32 a day, and the $49.99 renewal sits in 56 words of bullets under Subscribe. Print “$29.99 today, then $49.99 at renewal” on the Quarterly card, in one line.

    “Cut verbose, redundant text. One sentence where AI wrote three.” taste.md, S2, S4, S14, S15, S27

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Rise’s style. Top to bottom: a sticky countdown bar, a dial with the user’s result, a 2-line headline ending in violet, then 3 plan cards. Each shows the crossed-out price, the new price and a boxed per-day price. Tag them LITE VERSION, MOST POPULAR and BEST PRICE; preselect the middle plan with a violet outline. Discount the first period and print the renewal price on the card. Type: Work Sans, bold headlines, regular body. Colors: night black #17151A (60), electric violet #7D05FC (30) for the selection and button, sunrise orange #C56B3D (10) for the per-day price. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
