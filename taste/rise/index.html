Design style

Rise sets Work Sans on night-black #17151A screens, with grouped #22222A rows rounded at 12 pt. Electric violet #7D05FC marks tabs, tags and add buttons, while orange marks energy numbers and the high end of the energy curve. Store shots frame each phone in a violet-to-orange sunrise over purple hills.

Layout
Grouped lists inset 13 pt from the edges, with 56 pt rows, 1 pt separators and a 21 pt icon circle per row.
Shapes
Lists round at 12 pt, session cards at 13 pt and sheet tops at 28 pt; badges are full pills.
Color
Night black #17151A screens with #22222A rows; violet #7D05FC on tabs, tags and add buttons; orange #C56B3D on energy numbers.
Type
One family, Work Sans: Bold 700 headers, Medium 500 rows at 17 pt, Regular 400 big numbers like 6.3 hrs.
Imagery
All 8 store shots sit on a violet-to-orange sunrise; guided sessions use orange gradient art with 2 wave lines.
Components
An energy curve from violet #6F1DFE to orange #FC800C, violet Peak and Dip arrow tags, and a tab bar with violet active icons.

A prompt for Rise’s overall look: colors, type, shapes, layout and its signature components.

Restyle my app in Rise’s style. Keep my features, data and copy. Colors: - 60% night black #17151A: screens and sheets; rows and cards on #22222A. - 30% electric violet #7D05FC: active tabs, tags and add buttons; violet text #8C58FC. - 10% sunrise orange #C56B3D: energy numbers, peaks and session art. Type: - Headlines: Work Sans 700, about 21 pt, white over a grey Bold subline. - Body: Work Sans 400, 13 to 17 pt; Work Sans 500 for row labels; secondary text #BCBCC0. Shapes: - Grouped lists 12 pt corners, cards 13 pt, sheet tops 28 pt; badges are full pills. Layout: - Lists inset 13 pt, 56 pt rows with 1 pt #323335 separators, 21 pt icon circles. Components: - Energy chart: a curve that fades from violet #6F1DFE (low) to orange #FC800C (high), with a white now line. - Habit row: colored icon circle, label, bell and chevron on #22222A. - Session card: 13 pt corners, orange gradient art #4C0210 to #DF7001, a white play disc, a #22222A footer row. Icons and imagery: - White line icons; violet-to-orange sunrise gradients behind heroes and onboarding. Check: - Every screen background is #17151A or #000000. - Orange appears only on energy data and session art. - Lists use 12 pt corners and 56 pt rows.
