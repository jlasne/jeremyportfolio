Design style

Flat cartoon birds play on sky-blue skies and green grass, with white rounded cards for goals and speech bubbles. Loud fills mark big moments: pink for sending a Valentine, bright blue for the 30-day streak.

  • Sky blue and its pale tint cover 44% of the store shots
  • 8 of 8 headlines: 3 lines of dark blue Rubik Bold
  • White cards with corners about 24 px

How I’d improve it

  1. Put the term in every listing name

    9 listings read “Finch Plus” at 8 prices, $5.99 to $69.99, with $39.99 twice. Add the term to each, like “Finch Plus, 1 year”, and name what Guardian Program, $7.99, buys.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

  2. Take the grass labels to 4.5:1

    White “Goal Buddies”, “3 goals left today!” and “START THE DAY” sit on #7BB65C grass at 2.4:1. Set them in #101820, the icon’s eye color, for 7.4:1.

    “Text contrast: at least 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for text 24 px and larger.” taste.md, S27

  3. Swap “millions” for 776,001 ratings

    Screenshot 8 says “join millions growing with you”. Apple’s data showed 4.9 stars from 776,001 ratings on 15 Nov 2026. Write that number: “Rated 4.9 from 776,001 ratings”.

    “Make headlines specific. ‘Track 3 habits in 10 seconds a day’ beats ‘Transform your life’.” taste.md, S1, S2, S4, S16

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Finch’s style. Top to bottom: your mascot in a sky scene with a white speech bubble, a 3-line headline, white plan cards, one full-width button. Type: Rubik, bold headline in dark blue #1F4F9A, medium labels. Colors: sky blue #9DDDFF (60) for the screen, white #FFFFFF (30) for cards and the bubble, bright blue #0E9BE2 (10) for the button and the selected card’s outline. Button text #101820. Corners about 24 px. Plans: one tier in rows with length and price; preselect the longest and show its per-month price. Show a trial only if you offer one. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
