Design style

Finch builds cozy scenes: cartoon birds on sky-blue #9DDDFF skies and green grass, above white cards with 20 to 24 pt corners. Goals sit in 78 pt rows with an emoji tile, a bolt reward and a raised check button. Full-screen fills mark big moments: pink for a Valentine, bright blue #0E9BE2 for a 30-day streak.

Layout
16.5 pt side margins; a cartoon scene fills the top of the screen, and white cards stack below, 6.5 to 8 pt apart.
Shapes
Cards round at 20 to 24 pt, icon tiles are 54 pt squircles, and check buttons round at 13 pt with a raised edge.
Color
Sky blue #9DDDFF backdrops, white #FFFFFF cards and bubbles, dark text #2E2E2E; bright blue #0E9BE2 fills the streak screen.
Type
Rubik sets every word: Bold 700 for 3-line headlines and 21 pt titles, Medium 500 for 17 pt rows and speech bubbles.
Imagery
Flat cartoon birds appear on all 8 shots, with props like hearts, trophies and dumbbells.
Components
Goal rows with a raised check button, 7-dot habit sparklines at a 14.7 pt pitch, and a streak week of check coins.
