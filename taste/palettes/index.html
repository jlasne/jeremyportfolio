    How the 60/30/10 split works

    The palettes are starting points. Choose exact hex values that pass contrast, then judge them on the real screen.

    • Two colors do 90% of the work. The third one only goes on the button you want pressed.

      S5, @diego.a.does

    • Use 60/30/10: 60% main color, 30% second color, 10% accent. The 10% is what people click: buttons, the label, the main number.

      S31, @quanovaaiS32, @quanovaai

    • 1 accent per screen, picked for a reason. It must contrast with the base pair and fit the brand mood.

      S23, @charles.unemployed

    • Body text needs at least 4.5:1 contrast. Text 24 px and larger needs 3:1. Colors you must tell apart also differ in lightness.

      taste.md section 2, color rules

    • Apply one palette to the same screen 3 times to compare. Judge the result on the real design, not on swatches.

      S33, @quanovaai
