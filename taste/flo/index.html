My read

  • 3 goals get their own product name: Ovulation & Health tracker ($11.49, $14.99), Birth Control Reminder ($43.99), Pregnancy Tracker ($49.99). The receipt repeats why you came.
  • The first screenshot wears a green “FSA/HSA eligible” sticker, U.S. only. It turns Premium, up to $59.99, into a health expense paid with pre-tax money.
  • All 8 screenshots sell features, from period predictions to pregnancy. Prices, $1.99 to $59.99, show only in the In-App Purchases list. Value first, price later.

Design style

Lavender store screens frame white app screens, flat cartoon people and illustrated symptom icons. Feather pink #FF5A7D marks period days, card outlines and the Log period pill; only the sleep screen turns dark plum.

  • Lavender #E2CBFF fills 37% of the store screens
  • 8 of 8 headlines set line 2 in a pale pink box
  • Insight cards: 16 px corners, 3 px pink outlines

How I’d improve it

  1. Add the length to all 10 names

    6 listings share the name Flo Premium, from $1.99 to $59.99. Keep the goal names and add the term to each, like “Flo Premium, 1 year”.

    “Fix 3 things to stop a design from looking cheap: low contrast, a broken layout, and copy that does not convert.” taste.md, S27

  2. Darken the button pink to 4.6:1

    White “Log period” on #FF5A7D reads at 3.0:1. Fill the pill with #D9345E, the same hue, for 4.6:1, and keep #FF5A7D for the icon and period days.

    “Text contrast: at least 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for text 24 px and larger.” taste.md, S27

  3. Keep pink for period days and the button

    Screen 1 puts pink on 5 predicted days, the Log period pill and 2 card outlines. Drop the 3 px outlines from all 6 insight cards on screens 1, 2 and 4.

    “One accent per screen. Directing attention means one place to look.” taste.md, S8

Copy this style

Build a paywall in Flo’s style. Top to bottom: a medium sans line, then the key words large in a pale pink box with 10 px corners. Below: a white plan card and one pink pill button. Type: Inter 500 for text, Proza Libre for the boxed words. Colors: lavender #E2CBFF (60) for the screen, white #FFFFFF (30) for cards, feather pink #FF5A7D (10) for the button and the selected plan’s 3 px outline. Plans: one tier in rows with length and price; preselect the longest and show its per-month price. Show a trial only if you offer one. Use my brand colors in the same 60/30/10 roles.
