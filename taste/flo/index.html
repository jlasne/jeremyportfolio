Design style

Flo floats white cards on a blush #F7EFED home and lavender #E2CBFF store backdrops, with feather pink #FF5A7D for period data and the Log period pill. Insight cards pair a lavender or pink top with a white bottom inside a 3 pt pink outline. Flat illustrations and round symptom icons carry the imagery, and only the sleep screen turns dark plum.

Layout
About 22 pt side margins; a 7-day strip at a 51 pt pitch and a centered countdown top the home, then 2-column cards.
Shapes
Insight cards: about 15 pt corners, 3 pt pink outlines; calendar bands: 8.3 pt corners; Log period: a 39 pt pill.
Color
Lavender #E2CBFF backdrops, white #FFFFFF cards, feather pink #FF5A7D for period days, outlines and the pill; teal #1DA4A7 marks ovulation.
Type
A flared high-contrast face sets store key words; SF Pro sets the app, Bold 49 pt for the countdown, Semibold for titles.
Imagery
Flat illustrated people and hands appear on 3 of 8 shots; symptoms use round emoji-style icons in colored rings.
Components
A 7-day cycle strip with a 50 pt today circle, 2-color insight cards, and a month calendar with 41 pt period bands.
