Streak week

DuolingoProgress

A 124 pt number sits over 7 day circles, so a 3 day streak reads in 1 glance: 3 orange checks done, 4 gray days to go.

Rebuilt from App Store screenshot 6. See the source

Prompt

Build a streak week card like Duolingo’s. - Centered stack, 337 x 295 pt: number, label, week card. - Number: Nunito 800, 169 pt, glyph 124 x 75 pt, vertical gradient #FFB725 to #FFA10D. - Label “day streak”: Nunito 800, 34 pt, letter-spacing -0.01em, #FF9600, baseline 46 pt under the number. - Week card: 337 x 94 pt, 27 pt under the label, #FFFFFF fill, 1.9 pt #E5E5E5 border, 13.3 pt radius. - 7 columns at a 45.2 pt pitch. Day initials: Nunito 800, 16 pt, #AFAFAF; today’s initial turns #FF9600. - 10.5 pt under each initial, a 33.4 pt circle. Done: #FF9600 with a white 3.6 pt check, round caps. Upcoming: #E5E5E5. - Data rule: the number equals the run of days done. Fill 1 circle per day done, left to right. - When a day completes: pop its circle (scale 0.6 to 1.1 to 1, 300 ms), draw the check, then count the number up. Swap in my brand colors and font; keep the sizes.

Payattention

Severity gauge badge

FloBadges

Two of 3 arcs light up, green then orange, and an orange warning sign grades the result before the 2 words under it are read.

Rebuilt from App Store screenshot 5. See the source

Prompt

Build a severity gauge badge like Flo’s. - Card: 112 x 138 pt, #FFFFFF, 17.7 pt corners. - Gauge: 3 arcs on one circle, radius 41.3 pt, centered 56 pt from the top, stroke 8 pt, round caps, 5 degree gaps between arcs. - Arc angles, clockwise from 12 o clock: left 257 to 310, top 326 to 29.5, right 45.5 to 102.5 degrees. - Colors: left arc #65C16A to #C7B128 from bottom to top; top arc #E8AB12 to #FD7B01 from left to right; unlit arc #E6E6E6. - Center: 34 pt #FFF6E7 disc holding a 17 pt #FFA900 rounded triangle with a #FFFFFF exclamation mark. - Label: Pay attention in Inter 700, 16.5 pt, #000000, 2 centered lines, 20.4 pt line height, baselines at 100 and 120 pt. - States: light the arcs left to right by level, 1 to 3. Level 3 adds #F2453D. Sweep each arc in 300 ms, then pop the triangle from 0.8 to 1. Swap in my brand colors and font; keep the sizes.
