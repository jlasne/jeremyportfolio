The opportunity

Your audience is worth more than a sponsorship.

A sponsored post is attention you rent out once. An app is something you own — it earns every month, it compounds while you sleep, and it keeps paying long after the post has scrolled away.

ONE-OFF

Sponsored posts

Good money, zero ownership. The day you stop posting, it stops paying. You're renting your audience out by the hour.

THIN & HEAVY

Merch

Inventory, shipping, returns, sizing complaints. Margins get eaten by logistics and you inherit a warehouse problem.

SPIKES, THEN FADES

Courses

One big launch week, then a long decay. You have to keep making new ones to keep the number up.

AN ASSET

A mobile app

Recurring subscription revenue, no inventory, no fulfilment. It grows with every post you make and it's worth something the day you sell it.

The terms

Plainly, before you email me.

So there's no awkward call where it turns out we wanted different things.

You pay nothing

No retainer, no build fee, no invoice at any point. I carry the development cost and the development risk. If it never earns, I ate it.

Equity, not commission

You own a real share of the app itself — the asset, not a cut of this month's revenue. If it's ever sold, you're on that side of the table.

The split is negotiable

It depends on the app, the size and fit of your audience, and how much each of us is carrying. We agree it in writing before any code is written — never after.

I keep running it

This isn't a build-and-vanish. Support, OS updates, new features and the paywall work continue for as long as the app is live.

One at a time, properly

I take on a small number of these because each one is real ongoing work. If the fit isn't right I'll say so on the first call rather than waste a year of yours.

You don't need an idea

An audience and a clear sense of what they keep asking you for is enough. Working out what to build from that is part of my half.
