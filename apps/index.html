The lessons

6

October 2026

  1. Distribution

    Write like a friend, and creators answer

    My hand-written DMs beat my pitched ones. A thank-you and a joke from their world open the conversation.

  2. Distribution

    A break-up message got a reply in 34 minutes

    “If now isn’t the time, just tell me.” A silent creator answered within the hour.

  3. Distribution

    1 creator reply in 4 is a bot

    About a quarter of the replies were auto-messages. When a bot answers, I switch to the email in their bio.

  4. Distribution

    A page with their own numbers makes them reply

    A page at their name, with their real stats and their own words, did more than any pitch.

  5. Distribution

    Same DM from 2 accounts, trust gone

    A creator got my exact text twice and said “do better”. Each account now writes its own message.

  6. Distribution

    On WhatsApp, the first reply is a salesperson

    Business numbers answer with a bot or a seller. One line fixes it: I’m not a client, this is for the creator.
