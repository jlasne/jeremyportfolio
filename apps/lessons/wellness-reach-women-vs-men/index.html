Wellness creators: women 7x, men 1/3

Women creators: 7x their followers in views. Men get a third. That is the median, on the Instagram wellness accounts our pipeline pulled. Health, fitness and nutrition only, because that is where our leads are, and these numbers measure reach, not sales or conversion.

Why Instagram.

Every lead we contact comes from Instagram. We pull them with a pipeline, then reach out on every channel we find. Instagram is where creators are easiest to reach, and where views stay the most stable compared to followers. Stable views are what we look for.

Median views per follower.

So we compared the median views by type of account. The easiest split to make is women’s accounts versus men’s accounts. Women get 7 views per follower. Men get 1 view for every 3 followers. Put side by side, a woman with 10,000 followers reaches about 70,000 people, and a man with the same count reaches about 3,300.

A finding, not a theory.

Why the gap? Arguments go both ways, and I have no answer yet. This is an observation from our data, and for our outreach it changes one thing: in wellness, we judge a lead by its views per follower first.

Followers are a promise. Views are the proof.
