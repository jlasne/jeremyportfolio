I contact every influencer on every channel

Since the end of July, I contacted 4,000 influencers to promote my apps.

Here is the system. It starts once you picked the influencer, from a database or your own research.

One message is a coin flip.

Each person lives in one channel. Some read WhatsApp. Some read email. Some open only Instagram or TikTok.

Which one is theirs? You guess. Add the time zone, and your message lands at 3 a.m. under the morning’s notifications.

So I send during their day.

One person, every channel.

I write to the same person on every channel I find. Each channel has its own rule.

Instagram and TikTok: one shot.

On Instagram and TikTok, you get one message. Make it count.

The first 35 characters show in the inbox preview. They decide if the message gets opened.

I rewrote mine until they worked. My open rate went up 4x.

Email and WhatsApp: follow up.

Email and WhatsApp allow follow-ups. Use them.

Many people reply to a follow-up. They read it and feel they missed something.

One follow-up is too few. Here is my rhythm:

  • Day 0 The first message.
  • Day 3 Follow-up.
  • Day 7 Follow-up.
  • Day 30 Last follow-up.

I follow up only while they stay silent. A reply ends the sequence.

Trick 1: the email is always there.

Every influencer has a public email. You need to know where to look:

  • Instagram The email button on the profile.
  • YouTube The channel page, under “more”.
  • Their product The privacy policy of what they sell.

Trick 2: a few accounts.

Instagram and TikTok limit how many messages an account sends. So I run a few outreach accounts and grow them little by little.

Each account sends one message per person. On socials, your message competes with thousands of fans. Each account adds one more chance to be seen.

Volume, with respect.

The goal: volume, on every channel, all the time.

Respect sets the limit: no spam. One message per account. Four emails at most. A reply ends it.

More channels, more chances. More chances, more deals.
