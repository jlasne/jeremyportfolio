        How the log reads

        1. Intake and sport are read against the night and the morning after. Sleep and the watch are read against the same morning.
        2. Each side of a comparison, and each of the 3 time windows, needs 3 days. A finding then clears a luck check that holds chance findings to 1 in 10 on average.
        3. Cohen’s d is the gap between the two sides, in day-to-day spreads. A finding needs a d of 0.3 or more. From 0.8, the gap is large.
        4. For weight and resting HR, lower is better. For the other four results, higher is better.
        5. The Exp 0 log’s grids read as a whole from 21 fully logged days, with intake and body both in. Until then, each read is early.
        6. Sleep, recovery, HRV and resting HR come from the watch (Fitbit) each morning. Weight comes from the scale.
