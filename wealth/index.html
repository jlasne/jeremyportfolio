Risk and danger

Movement against zero

Risk is how much the price moves in a year. Danger is the chance the capital goes to zero, 0 to 100, read per country. Accept the first, refuse the second. Risk is measured on the fat layer, where daily prices exist.

Crash ladder

How far a crash has to spread

A crash starts local. Each step up is rarer than the one below. The bar shows the largest share of your assets one crash would hit. Energy and war crashes cut across scales: the cushion is what gains when they hit.

Study tool for your own structure, not investment advice. Asset, country and correlation readings come from /invest, read 2 Oct 2026. Exchange rates: ECB reference, 14 Oct 2026; are estimates.

Country groups for the crash ladder (region, continent, West, East, hinge) are a working reading and can be argued with.
