Wealth Architecture

The Country Map

Thirty-two countries, read three ways.

Where you invest matters more than most people expect. A company is not just a company — it sits inside an economy, which has its own weather, its own debts, and its own chance of going badly wrong. This page asks three questions of every country, because no single question is enough.

1

What weather is it in?

Charles Gave

Is the economy growing? Are prices rising? Those two questions make four combinations, and each one rewards owning something different.

2

Where is it on the long arc?

Ray Dalio

Countries rise, peak, decline and reset over centuries. A country early in that arc and one late in it are not the same investment, even if this year's numbers look alike.

3

Volatile, or actually deadly?

Richard Détente

A price that jumps around is not the same as money that never comes back. Countries with calm-looking finances have gone to zero. Countries that look alarming have not.

The long arc

Every country is somewhere on the same curve: building strength, at its peak, declining under its debts, or rebuilding after a reset. This is a rough placement, not a schedule — you cannot date it precisely, only read the health.
