04

How to read it

  • The morning afterA day is judged by the night that follows it. A drink on Tuesday is scored against Wednesday's sleep, never Tuesday's.
  • High against lowAn on/off thing splits on itself. A counted thing splits at my own median, so "high coffee" means high for me.
  • Green is good, not positiveA resting heart rate going down is a win, so it turns green while the number falls.
  • The strength labelHow far apart the two groups sit, measured against how much that number swings day to day anyway. A big percent on a wild metric says less than a small one on a steady metric.
  • What stays hiddenA cell needs 4 days on each side before it prints. The whole matrix stays shut until 21 days carry both the taps and the body numbers.
  • One month only1 to 31 October 2026. Thirty-one days, one video a day, then the table stands as it is.
