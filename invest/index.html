The frame

Gave's 4 seasons

Two dials, four squares. Growth runs across, inflation runs up. Each square has assets that win there.

Country data: Trading Economics, read 2 to 3 October 2026. Real correlations, antifragility tests and 5-year figures: Polygon weekly closes via Grok for 40 assets, October 2021 to October 2026, in US dollars, through US-listed ETFs. Antifragility: curve of each asset's weekly return against the world index (VT) and its square, plus return against 4-week world turbulence. Estimated correlations come from shared drivers (stocks, oil, rates, debasement, AI, China, emerging-market currencies, safe havens, the dollar). They land within 0.12 of the real values on average.
